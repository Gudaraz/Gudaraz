## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a Licensed Attorney, Legal Engineer and Software Developer specializing in legal operations transformation, process automation, legal technology implementation and AI governance.

As an Independent Legal Operations & LegalTech Consultant, I design and build systems—from workflow architecture and automated document generation to proprietary RAG deployments—bridging legal, compliance, business and technical teams throughout the full implementation cycle.

- 🗣️ **Languages:** Spanish (Native) · English (C1) · Russian (B2)
- 🇪🇺 **Mobility:** EU Citizen (Spanish passport) · Open to relocation within Europe
- ⚖️ **Focus:** Legal Operations · LegalTech · AI Governance · Process Automation · Data Sovereignty
- 📋 **Project Management:** Google Project Management Professional Certificate, September 2026

## 🏆 Key Milestones & Impact

- 🏅 **Award-winning innovation:** Designed and developed **Younomad**, a legal settlement platform for digital nomads in Spain. Winner of the 2025 *Concurso de Innovación Jurídica* by Fundación Mutualidad de la Abogacía in Madrid, including €10,000 in seed capital.

- ⚡ **80% efficiency improvement:** Redesigned intestate succession workflows and developed automation at Total Abogados, reducing case-processing time from **60 to 15 minutes per case**.

- 🧠 **Enterprise AI & RAG:** Developed implementation designs for RAG-based compliance knowledge assistants for global organizations, including technical architecture, model-selection criteria, delivery planning, UAT governance, GDPR compliance and EU AI Act alignment.

- 📄 **CLM development:** Co-developed **TAPP Legal**, a B2B contract-lifecycle management platform enabling HR departments to manage employment-contract lifecycles through guided workflows and document automation.

## 🌱 Current Focus

- **AI Governance & RAG:** Advising on EU AI Act risk classification, GDPR operational requirements, privacy-by-design and data-sovereignty architectures.

- **Legal Operations Transformation:** Redesigning legal service delivery through workflow optimization, process mapping, document automation, technology evaluation and measurable ROI.

- **Process Design:** Translating legal and operational requirements into BPMN diagrams, decision matrices, business rules, user stories and API blueprints.

- **Project Delivery:** Applying Agile, Scrum, Kanban, stakeholder management and UAT governance to legal-tech implementation projects.

- **Software Development:** Building full-stack capability with Python, Django, PostgreSQL, JavaScript, React and Node.js.
